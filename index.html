A Sleep-Dependent Plasticity in Mouse S1 Master's thesis · Larkum Lab, Charité +
Question

How does sleep reshape neural circuits in the somatosensory cortex — and which sleep states do the work?

Approach

Two-photon calcium imaging combined with EEG/EMG recording across wake, NREM and REM. Own data acquisition, quality control and analysis design; a standardised processing framework for heterogeneous multi-source data.

Output

An end-to-end MATLAB & Python pipeline — cleaning, statistical modelling, visualisation and written reporting — providing the data basis for project decisions.
